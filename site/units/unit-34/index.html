Vocabulary

  • Dada — an artistic and literary movement formed in response to World War I and modern machine culture
  • Readymade — Duchamp’s term for art made from manufactured objects
  • Surrealism — a movement that emphasized dreams and the unconscious
  • Automatism — a Surrealist practice of writing or making art while reducing deliberate conscious control
