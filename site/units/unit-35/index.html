Vocabulary

  • Modernism — a broad cultural movement that sought new forms suited to modern experience
  • Orphism — an abstract, Cubist-influenced style developed by Robert and Sonia Delaunay around 1912
  • Suprematism — Malevich’s term for abstract painting that abandoned reference to the visible world
