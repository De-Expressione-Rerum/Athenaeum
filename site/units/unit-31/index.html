Vocabulary

  • Cubism — a new approach to representing reality developed by Picasso and Braque around 1907–08
  • Collage — a work or technique that combines attached pieces of paper, photographs, fabric, or other materials
  • Calligram — a poem in which words are arranged on the page to work visually as well as verbally
